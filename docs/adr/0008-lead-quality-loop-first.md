# Prove the lead quality loop first

The first MVP will prioritize proving that the system can select content leads the reader wants to inspect further. After Source Enrichment, local Codex Curation will evaluate Raw Items in schema-constrained batches of at most twenty against current evidence, Editorial Memory, and the Reference Library, replacing URL and keyword heuristics as the authority for Lead Scores and rejection decisions. A failed batch is retried twice and then remains Awaiting Curation rather than falling back to heuristic output; the Daily Editorial Board may contain fewer items and be partial. The MVP will use a small number of controllable ingestion sources before adding harder integrations such as tracked social accounts, video intake, or heavier automation.
