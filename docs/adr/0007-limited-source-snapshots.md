# Store limited source snapshots by default

The system will store limited source snapshots instead of full copyrighted articles by default. It may follow primary editorial links, resolve tracking redirects, and extract enough main text to identify claims and produce grounded summaries, but it will not bypass paywalls, login requirements, or CAPTCHA; when extraction fails, newsletter text or provider excerpts remain usable with lower confidence. Article evidence is capped at roughly 12,000 characters and Newsletter Story evidence at roughly 6,000 characters, with evidence text retained for 90 days before deletion while attribution, claims, fingerprints, and processing metadata remain for deduplication. Material explicitly promoted to the Reference Library is retained until the reader removes it; full content storage is otherwise reserved for user-uploaded materials or sources with explicit rights.
